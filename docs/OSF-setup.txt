【Description 描述】
复制以下英文粘贴到 OSF 项目页的 Description 栏：

Linna is a personal knowledge management system that combines (1) a 3D spatial universe as the organizing metaphor for personal knowledge, (2) a personal knowledge base called the "Book of the Universe" that grows with the user over time, and (3) an AI assistant that retrieves answers from this personal knowledge base rather than from generic internet training data. The core insight: a personal AI must have personal knowledge. The long-term vision is a carrier platform — an application that evolves from a personal knowledge tool into an indispensable daily AI companion.
